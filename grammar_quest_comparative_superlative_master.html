Primary 4 Adjective Rules

Category A

Short Adjectives (+er / +est)

Add -er than for comparative and the ...-est for superlative.

Examples: cheap → cheaper than → the cheapest | tall → taller than → the tallest

Category B

Ending in -e (+r / +st)

Just add -r than or the ...-st.

Examples: brave → braver than → the bravest | cute → cuter than → the cutest

Category C

Double Final Consonant

Short vowel + single consonant → double the last letter!

Examples: big → bigger than → the biggest | hot → hotter than → the hottest | thin → thinner than → the thinnest

Category D

Ending in -y (→ -ier / -iest)

Change -y into -ier than or the ...-iest.

Examples: happy → happier than → the happiest | dirty → dirtier than → the dirtiest

Category E

Long Adjectives (2-syllables non -y / 3+ syllables)

Use more ... than or the most ... before the adjective!

Examples: beautiful → more beautiful than → the most beautiful | dangerous → more dangerous than → the most dangerous

Category F

Irregular Adjectives

Special forms that change completely!

Examples: good → better than → the best | bad → worse than → the worst
